## Key facts

| Topic | Field | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tenancy_law | Main law | Civil Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan, property lease provisions (Art. 675 and following), including special rules for lease of residential rooms. | [Official Legal Acts Database of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az, Ministry of Justice)](https://e-qanun.az/framework/46944) |
| termination | Termination notice (residential) | A lease of a residential room may be terminated only by notice given on the first working day of a calendar month, taking effect at the end of the following month; notice must be in writing and state the grounds (Arts. 690.1, 690.2, 691.2). The text extends the notice periods in the tenant's favour by three months after three, six and nine years of occupation (Art. 691.3). | [Official Legal Acts Database of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az, Ministry of Justice)](https://e-qanun.az/framework/46944) |
| termination | Landlord grounds | For a residential room the landlord may terminate only where there is a substantial interest, for example the tenant is at fault for breaching contract obligations or the landlord needs the room as a home for self or family members (Art. 692.1, 692.2). An agreement allowing the landlord to terminate immediately on grounds other than those in law has no effect (Art. 687). | [Official Legal Acts Database of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az, Ministry of Justice)](https://e-qanun.az/framework/46944) |
| deposit | Security cap | Where the tenant must give security for performance of the lease, it may not exceed twice the monthly rent; cash security must be kept by the landlord separately from own assets in a bank at the interest usual for savings deposits, the interest belonging to the tenant (Art. 688.2). For residential rooms an agreement departing from this to the tenant's detriment has no effect (Art. 688.3). | [Official Legal Acts Database of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az, Ministry of Justice)](https://e-qanun.az/framework/46944) |
| deposit | Reservation payment | A reservation payment made to the landlord before the lease is credited against rent when the lease is concluded; the part exceeding two months of rent can be reclaimed by the person at any time (Art. 688.1). A landlord of a residential room cannot validly agree a forfeit-type payment from the tenant (Art. 688.4). | [Official Legal Acts Database of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az, Ministry of Justice)](https://e-qanun.az/framework/46944) |
| rent_payment | When rent is due | Where the lease term is longer than one month, rent is paid in instalments at the end of each month; rent set by periods is paid at the end of each period (Art. 684.1). | [Official Legal Acts Database of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az, Ministry of Justice)](https://e-qanun.az/framework/46944) |

## Method

1. Each value comes from a primary source: the statute on the official legal portal, a government service page or a statistics office dataset. Where a second official or institutional page confirms it, that link is in `cross_check_url`.
2. Values are written in our own words. Nothing is estimated or calculated; where no verified value exists the cell is empty and the reason is given in `sources.md`.
3. Where a statute is translated or summarised from another language, the original-language text prevails.
